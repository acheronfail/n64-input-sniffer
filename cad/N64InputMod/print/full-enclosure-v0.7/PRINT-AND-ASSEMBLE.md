# N64InputMod v0.7 - fasteners from the owner's stock

The complete Orca project contains 12 print parts on three plates: lower shell, upper shell, keeper, four rigid shoes, four flexible pads and an optional translucent window. Select your actual filament profiles before slicing. The lower shell prints floor-down and the lid roof-down.

## Fasteners

| Fastener | Quantity | Purpose |
|---|---:|---|
| Normal M2 x 8 mm screws suitable for plastic | 2 | Existing board keeper |
| M2 x 20 mm hex-socket flat-head machine screws | 6 | Enclosure closure |
| M2 hex nuts, nominal 4 mm across flats and 1.6 mm thick | 6 | Side-loading lid pockets |
| M3 x 8 mm hex-socket flat-head machine screws | 4 | Male connector adjustment |
| M3 hex nuts, nominal 5.5 mm across flats and 2.4 mm thick | 4 | Captive clamp pockets |

No brass inserts or tapping are needed. Only the keeper screws thread directly into plastic; reuse the tested M2 x 8 mm screws. The M2 x 12 mm screws are not the specified replacement. Keeper head clearance remains based on a head no larger than 4 mm diameter x 1.7 mm high.

The flat-head seats assume 90-degree metric heads, nominal 4 mm diameter for M2 and 6 mm for M3. Exact purchased head dimensions have not been provided; this is not a universal ISO 10642 or imperial countersink. Seat mouths are 4.2 and 6.4 mm respectively. Flat-head lengths include the head.

The closure screws pass through clearance holes and engage six metal M2 nuts. Each nut pocket has 4.3 mm across-flat clearance and a 2 mm height. Its floor is 1.84 mm thick above the lid's mating surface. A nominal M2 x 20 screw reaches Z=4 mm, engaging the complete 1.6 mm nut at Z=2–3.6 mm. The earlier M2.5 closure screws are superseded.

The M3 clamp screws remain adjusters. Their heads may stand proud: about 1.2 mm above the roof at the nominal uncompressed pad/shoe stack. Adjust for connector grip, not for a flush head. Countersinks provide clearance and must not be used as the clamp adjustment stop.

## Assembly

1. Fit the board on the raised pedestal and secure the unchanged keeper with two normal M2 x 8 screws.
2. With the lid removed, slide six M2 nuts into its closure pockets from the interior. Load the two outer rear pockets toward the rear from the front; the other four load outward from the centre of the enclosure. Use tweezers if helpful and keep the nuts seated while bringing the halves together.
3. Install four female and four male connectors. Route wires clear of closure posts and clamp shoes. Place one soft 12 x 8 x 0.8 mm pad and one rigid shoe on each male housing.
4. Fit four M3 nuts in the lid's clamp pockets from underneath. Retract the four M3 x 8 flat-head screws before lowering the lid.
5. Close the enclosure with six M2 x 20 flat-head screws from underneath. Tighten gently against the plastic countersinks.
6. Adjust the M3 screws until the connectors resist sliding. Fit the optional translucent window with a little adhesive around its edge as needed.

The raised board, split USB opening, support braces and tapered LED ledge are retained. Review the sliced preview, particularly the short bridges over side-loading nut pockets. Hardware seats and physical assembly still need a print check.
