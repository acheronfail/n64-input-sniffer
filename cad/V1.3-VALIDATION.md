## Final geometry

- Stripped male body: 18.5 mm diameter, 14.3 mm flat-to-arc height, 18 mm rear length and 29.9 mm overall length.
- Enclosure: approximately 168.591 × 58.130 × 32 mm; 15.5 mm shallower than v1.2.
- Printable solid volume: 148,528 mm³, 17.8% below v1.2; this is not a filament estimate.
- Minimum paired connector gap: 18.094 mm; minimum opposing keeper-tower gap: 2.094 mm.
- Cradle: 1.5 mm extra depth. Side-tab pockets and the 7 mm axial shoulder clearance are retained.
- Keeper: nominal 1.9 mm side walls brace the front face. The additional 10 × 6.5 mm central tier is omitted; the wider internal step remains.

The owner first confirmed the manually trimmed keeper’s fit and clamping pressure, then confirmed the complete final enclosure. The last geometry comparison showed removal of only 141.7 mm³ from each of the four small tiers; all other printable production parts were unchanged.

## Digital checks completed

FreeCAD 1.1.3 and OrcaSlicer 2.4.2 were used. Checks passed for valid connected solids, assembly clearances, keeper retention and insertion, screw paths, lid removal, USB access, complete nut-pocket walls, preserved wider steps, absence of the small tiers and watertight meshes.

The released 3MF matched the generated print-oriented STLs and verified toolpaths. Its delivered hash was checked again before release cleanup. The archive contains 12 parts on three plates, using the Bambu Lab P1S 0.4 mm profile, Supertack bed, 0.16 mm layers and a 0.20 mm first layer:

No supports are used. Plate 1 pauses before layer 88 at Z = 14.12 mm for six M2 nuts; all six pocket caps begin after the pause. Plates 2 and 3 have no pauses. The inherited bed/filament temperature warning is unchanged; no new warnings were accepted.
