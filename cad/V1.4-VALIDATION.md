## Side closure change

The outer closure bolts and captive nuts move from (X, Y) = (±79.5, 2) mm to (±79.5, −13.8) mm, a 15.8 mm shift along each side. At the retained X positions, the side outline spans Y = −40.899 to +13.360 mm; its midpoint is −13.770 mm. The rounded placement is 0.0305 mm from that midpoint. The four other closure bolts retain their positions.

The old bolt holes are filled. The new bosses retain the original radius and nut-pocket height. Local relief around the outer male keeper bridges and post edges clears the relocated bosses without changing the connector contact surfaces.

Relative to v1.3, only the lower shell, upper shell and male keepers 1 and 4 change. All other production parts and hardware references are geometrically identical to v1.3. Reprint those four parts together; the complete project includes all 12 parts for a fresh build.

The fasteners are geometrically centred and the owner has confirmed the physical fit. Pressure distribution has not been measured independently.



## Upper-shell rib reinforcement

The screenshot identified paired thin walls separated by empty slots between adjacent connector collars. All four such slots are now filled: male and female rows, on the left and right sides of the board bay. The 0.84 mm male-row and 1.30 mm female-row gaps become continuous nominal ribs 1.0 mm and 1.66 mm wide, including the existing wall skins.

The comparison against the first v1.4 prototype confirms that only the upper shell changes: 984.56 mm³ is added and no material is removed. The lower shell and every separate printed part remain identical to that prototype. If the first v1.4 parts are already printed, only the upper shell needs replacing.

Checks passed for filled rib cross-sections, all four complete bridges, the unchanged enclosure outline, clearance from the lower shell, and unobstructed vertical removal paths for all keepers. Existing assembly, connector, fastener, mesh and toolpath checks also passed. The regenerated slice uses no supports and retains the verified nut-pause ordering. The owner has confirmed the reinforced version as working well.
